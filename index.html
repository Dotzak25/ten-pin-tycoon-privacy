Ten Pin Tycoon

Privacy Policy. Effective October 2026.

Ten Pin Tycoon ("the app") is made by OneKindMedia. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (for example cash, lanes, balls, scores and settings) is saved only on your own device so you can continue playing. It never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third-party services

The app works offline and does not connect to any third-party services. If a future version adds optional features such as leaderboards through Apple Game Center, this policy will be updated first.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

Support and contact

Questions, bug reports or feedback: jason@okm.ltd. We usually reply within a few days.
